# 🎙️ Voice-Controlled Expense & Financial Tracker (Android)

An intelligent, voice-powered Android application designed to effortlessly manage your daily finances, expenses, incomes, installments (loans), and checks. Simply speak, and let the app handle the bookkeeping for you!

---

## ✨ Features

*   **🗣️ Voice-Activated Input:** Record your transactions, expenses, and incomes instantly using voice commands without tedious typing.
*   **💰 Expense & Income Management:** Keep track of where your money goes and where it comes from with categorized logs.
*   **📅 Installment (Loan) Tracker:** Never miss a loan payment or installment deadline again.
*   **📝 Check Management:** Keep a structured record of issued and received checks, along with due dates.
*   **📊 Financial Reports:** Get clear insights into your current account balance and financial health.
*   **⚡ Lightweight & Fast:** Designed for a smooth and responsive user experience on Android devices.

## 📥 Download & Installation

You can download the ready-to-use APK directly from the releases page:

1. Go to the [Releases Page](../../releases/latest).
2. Download the latest `app-release.apk` file to your Android device.
3. Install the APK (make sure to enable "Install from unknown sources" in your device settings if prompted).
4. Open the app and start managing your finances with your voice!

---

## ⚠️ Requirements

*   **OS:** Android 6.0 (Marshmallow) or higher.
*   **Permissions:** Microphone access (required for voice-to-text bookkeeping).
